03 ✻ The Maker & His Medium

Obsessed with ink on paper, halftone grain, and wearable culture.

Hello! I’m Rupam Nandan, an independent graphic designer and screen printer. My creative world sits at the intersection of analog printing presses, indie record sleeves, and high-impact streetwear.

I love the tangible imperfections of physical printmaking: the microscopic texture of cotton paper, the slight registration misalignment of a two-drum risograph, and hand-pulled waterbased inks that age beautifully with every wash.

05 ✻ Studio Methodology

From Concept to Tangible Print

A reliable, four-step production pipeline honed through hundreds of commercial screen pulls and print runs.

01 Phase I

Brief & Moodboard

Defining your audience, garment base cuts or paper weights, ink count restrictions, and compiling visual references.

✓ Strategy & Specs
02 Phase II

Concept & Sketches

Drafting three distinct compositional directions, typography pairings, and rough thumbnail illustrations for your initial review.

✓ Direction Lock
03 Phase III

Design & Refinement

Vectorizing line art, calculating halftone frequency, generating riso traps, and refining typography through collaborative feedback.

✓ Vector Polish
04 Phase IV

Production & Delivery

Packaging 300 DPI master files, color-separated print PDFs, Pantone color cards, and manufacturer technical callouts.

✓ Press-Ready Files
